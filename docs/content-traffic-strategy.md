# Ruang content traffic strategy

Ruang turns a brief into a measurable content system, not a promise of
virality. The AI receives a versioned playbook, the Brand Brain, and the latest
30-day performance signals. It proposes platform-native drafts and experiments;
a human still approves every item before it enters the publishing queue.

## Evidence base

- [Google Search Central: helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)
  anchors the workflow in originality, usefulness, trust, and clear “who, how,
  and why”—not mass content made only to manipulate rankings.
- [Google Trends FAQ](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en)
  explains that Trends reports normalized relative interest. Ruang therefore
  treats it as a comparative signal, not absolute demand or polling.
- [Google Trends related searches](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4355000?hl=en)
  supports using top and rising searches as topic hypotheses.
- [TikTok Creative Center Trends](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-use-trends)
  informs the short-video workflow: inspect region, industry, timeframe,
  related videos, hashtags, and audience—not a trend label in isolation.
- [LinkedIn thought leadership](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/resources/marketing-terms/thought-leadership)
  informs original points of view, persona relevance, expertise, and
  repurposing one useful idea into multiple formats.
- [X organic best practices](https://business.x.com/en/basics/organic-best-practices)
  informs concise conversational copy, a clear CTA, media use, content
  calendars, and test-learn-test iterations.
- [X post activity dashboard](https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-measurement-and-analytics/tweet-activity-dashboard)
  informs the X feedback loop for impressions, engagements, clicks, replies,
  reposts, and profile actions.

## Operating model

1. **Demand hypothesis** — combine a real audience problem with operator-supplied
   topic seeds or current trend research. Ruang does not fabricate trend volume.
2. **Original answer** — use Brand Brain expertise, product knowledge, proof, and
   constraints to produce a useful answer.
3. **Native distribution** — adapt the core idea to each platform's role, hook,
   format, character limits, and CTA.
4. **Conversion path** — assign one desired action and one primary success signal
   to every item.
5. **Controlled experiment** — vary a hook, format, or CTA while keeping the rest
   stable enough to learn.
6. **Feedback loop** — feed 30-day analytics into the next AI generation. Keep
   winners, diagnose weak stages, and document the next hypothesis.

The strategy snapshot and source list are stored on each campaign for audit and
reproducibility. Because external trends change, current trend observations must
be placed in Brand Brain topic seeds or the campaign brief and revalidated by a
human.
